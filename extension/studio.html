GET STARTED

A little more room to explore.

Open any website in Chrome, then click the DuoView extension icon. Your current page opens in both views.

  • Navigate independently. Each view has Back, Forward, Reload, and its own address bar. The top address bar opens a URL in all views.
  • Try different sizes. Pick a device, enter custom dimensions, rotate, or adjust the display scale. Fit keeps the entire viewport visible.
  • Pick up where you left off. Device settings and the last URL in each view are saved on this computer. No account needed.

When a site won't load

Compatibility mode removes X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy response headers inside this preview tab. Use it for sites you trust. It is off by default and resets when the workspace reopens.

Sign-in flows, third-party cookie restrictions, and frame-busting scripts may still require Open in tab ↗. Mobile view tests responsive CSS; it does not emulate a phone's hardware or mobile browser.

Back and Forward reopen the URLs in that view's history. Unsaved form values, POST requests, scroll position, and in-memory app state are not restored. Views share your browser's website storage when Chrome permits it.
